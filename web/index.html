    How does it work?

    Bluetooth radios do not report a direction, only signal strength. Your body absorbs a lot of Bluetooth signal, so if you hold the phone in front of your chest and turn around slowly, the signal is strongest when you face the device. BT Finder combines those readings with your phone's compass to estimate the direction, and uses the signal strength for a rough distance.

    Only Bluetooth Low Energy devices that are advertising can be found. Many devices stop advertising while they are connected to a phone or computer, so disconnect them first.

    How to find the direction
    1. Hold the phone flat in front of your chest, top edge pointing forward.
    2. Turn slowly on the spot, a full circle in about 15 seconds.
    3. The arrow points where the signal was strongest. The shaded wedge shows how sure it is.
    4. Walk that way a few metres, then turn around again. Use the trend and distance to tell when you are close.
